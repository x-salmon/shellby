### New

- The local model writes more of Shellby's drafts: hook and workflow drafts as well as routines and learned rules, and Quiz me questions if you turn them on.
- Settings → Local model lists what it writes first. Turn each job on or off, or give it a model of its own, such as a tiny one for rule wording and a bigger one for workflows.
- Settings shows what the local model has saved: drafts written on this PC, roughly how many tokens your plan didn't spend, how long each took, and how often Claude stepped in.
- When Claude steps in, Settings says why: the model took too long, wasn't running, or answered in the wrong shape. A model that's left out for a while can be tried again at once.
- Try it: ask the local model anything from Settings and see its answer and how long it took, without using your plan.
- Detect finds Ollama or LM Studio on their usual ports and lists their models in one click.
