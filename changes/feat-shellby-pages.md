### New
- **Pages: small apps Claude builds for you, that live in Shellby.** Describe one (a print queue, a reading list, a board your helper crabs post to) and Claude builds it in a conversation, with a live preview beside the chat. It's a draft until you press Save to my pages. Your pages sit on the new Pages screen (Ctrl+9), open full size or beside a chat, and turn up in Ctrl+K.
- **Your agents can write to them.** Every conversation, routine and terminal running Claude Code can read a page and add to it, so three helper crabs can tick off one checklist and a morning routine can fill a dashboard. Each write is signed with who made it, and a page can say who may write: any conversation, only Shellby's own, or nobody.
- **Safe by design.** A page runs in a sandbox with no way onto the network or into Shellby, and can do only what you let it: read your usage, ask Claude something, notify you, or fetch from web addresses it names. You see all of that in plain words before it first runs, and everything every page can do is in Settings › Claude › Pages, with a switch to turn them all off.
- **Every save is a version.** A page's History keeps its last 20 versions, with who changed it and why, and Go back to this one. When a page breaks, Fix it with Claude hands Claude the error, and Go back to the last version puts the working one back.
- **Pages on your phone.** The phone app has a Pages tab: a page made for a phone opens full screen with its data live from your PC, and every call it makes is checked on the PC. One built for the desk has Change with Claude to make it fit. Pinned pages sit on the phone's Beach, a page's notifications reach the phone, and while the PC sleeps a page shows how it last looked.
- **Pinned pages in the conversations list.** Up to three pinned pages sit in the open-conversations list (Ctrl+Shift+A), after what's waiting on you.
