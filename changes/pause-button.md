### New
- **Pause, beside Stop.** Pause lets Claude finish the step he's on, then holds him there: nothing is cut off halfway, and his helpers pause too. The line under the box says what he's still finishing ("Pausing after this step… waiting for Bash") until he's paused, then Pause turns into Continue. Anything you type while he's paused goes in when you continue, so it's a clean way to set him straight mid-task. Pause and Continue are in the command menu too.
- **Pauses keep their cache.** A pause that runs close to the end of the conversation's one-hour cache has Claude note where he stopped and end the turn, which keeps the cache warm for another hour. Keep warm then looks after it like any idle conversation, and Continue picks up where he left off, or Stop ends the pause if you won't carry on. A pause that a helper is holding can't do that without ending the helper's work, so its line says when the cache goes cold instead.
