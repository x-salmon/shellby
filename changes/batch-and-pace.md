### New
- **Messages typed close together go to Claude as one turn.** Type a thought in three Enters and Shellby waits a moment and a half for company, says so in a chip above the box ("Sending in a moment…", with Send now), and sends them together, each its own paragraph with its files. Messages that pile up behind a running turn go together when it ends, too. Every turn saved is usage kept. Enter on an empty box or Ctrl+Enter sends now; a /command always goes on its own. Settings → Limits turns it off.
- **Routines wait when your 5-hour window is nearly full.** A routine due while the window is at 85% or more, past the share you keep for yourself, or on pace to fill before a run could finish, waits for the reset and runs then, once. The Routines page says "waiting for the reset" and when, and so does he. "Run even near the limit" in the routine editor lets one run anyway.
