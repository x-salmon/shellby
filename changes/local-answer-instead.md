### New
- **Ask your local model when Claude won't answer.** Turn it on in Settings → Local model, and when Claude declines a question (or the API's safety check stops the reply), a card under it offers to ask a model on your PC instead. It can have its own model, apart from the one that writes drafts. Only your question goes to it, only when you press the button, and its answer is marked as the local model's and kept out of Claude's conversation.

### Fixed
- **The model's name no longer spills over the effort chip in a narrow panel.** The bar above the conversation now fits: the model shows by name ("Sonnet 5.5"), cut short with "…" when space runs out, and the meters get more compact.
