### New
- **Point at your dev server.** A running dev server's card has **Point at it**: your site opens in a Shellby window, you click the part you want changed, say what should change, and it goes to Claude in that project with a picture of it. It lands in the conversation you're in there, or a new one. Press F2 to point again. The window only ever shows your localhost server, and links elsewhere open in your browser.
- **Say what should change when you point.** Point at part of a page and its card has a box for what you want, so Claude starts on it straight away instead of asking first. A picture of what you picked goes along too.
- **Point at a draft while Claude builds it.** In a draft page, what you point at goes to the conversation building it, as your message.
- **Point at several parts at once.** Shift+click picks up to five, numbered as you go, then click or press Enter to finish.

### Changed
- While you point, the part under the pointer shows its name, the outline stays on it as the page scrolls, and Escape stops pointing.
