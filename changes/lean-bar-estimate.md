### Changed
- **The Lean tab draws what every call carries as one bar.** "Before your first word" says the total, its share of a 200k window and when it was measured, then one stacked bar coloured by part: Claude Code's own prompt and tools, MCP tool lists, plugins, skill descriptions, CLAUDE.md and rules, the output style, hooks. Under it, a row per part with its tokens, a small share bar, "N% of a turn", and the cut on offer with what it saves: Turn off for the output style or an idle plugin (asked first, as before), Draft it for a CLAUDE.md section to move or a hook to quieten, which opens a prompt in a new tab for you to read and send. MCP tool lists get advice only: Shellby can't size them, and a per-tool cut is a `permissions.deny` rule Claude can draft for you. A card beside the rows adds it up, "If you take all N: now → after", and one button works through them in turn, each switch through its confirm window and the advice as one draft. Nothing is written by itself. "What the cache does" sits under it with this week's cache rate. The turn footer's "setup ~Nk/turn" line stays.
- **The run estimate sits on the button now.** With Ultracode on, or a message that asks for several helpers, the Send button reads "Send · ~8% of your window", and the line under the box has a small meter and "about 8% of your 5-hour window · it would end about 62% full · resets 4:15". The Ultracode strip adds "Runs like this took 5% to 12% of your 5-hour window before. Yours is 54% full." A workflow's Run button reads "Run · ~3% of your window" and runs in one tap: the box that asked for a second tap is gone. Past your limit, either button turns amber and offers to send or run after the reset instead; Enter still sends.
