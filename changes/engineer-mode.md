### New
- **Engineer mode.** Turn on the **Engineer** chip under the box and Claude decides before it builds. It explores, then puts its decision on a card: the goal, what limits it, the options it weighed with their pros and cons, its pick and why, how it'll test it, and the risks. Approve it, go with another option, or send it back with a note. Nothing touches your files until you approve one. Then Claude writes the decision into `docs/decisions` for whoever comes next, builds it, runs the test plan, and the same card says whether the work stuck to the decision and how each check went.
