### New

- Shellby on your phone works on a tablet or a computer's browser too: on a wide screen the beach stays on the left and the conversation you pick opens beside it, with the open one marked in the list. Turn a tablet or resize the window and it lays itself out again.
- Once a conversation runs on Codex, Gemini CLI or OpenCode, every conversation on your phone shows which agent it runs on, with a coloured mark, on its card and at the top of the conversation.
- With other PCs in the hub, Shellby on your phone shows them beside this PC at the top. Tap one to open that PC's own Shellby; pair it once there, and it keeps its own rules.
