### New

- Conversations know about each other. Claude can list the other conversations open in the same project, read what was last said in one, and hand one of them, or a new conversation, a brief to start on. It only ever sees the project it's working in.
- A hand-off shows you the brief as a card first, and nothing is sent until you say so. Turn that off with "Ask me before a hand-off" in Settings. The card still shows whenever the other conversation runs in a mode that can do more without asking.
- The conversation that gets the brief is told which branch and commit the sender is on, and warned about any changes the sender hasn't committed yet, since those are only in the sender's own copy.
