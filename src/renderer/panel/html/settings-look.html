Mischief

Off unless you turn it on. A cheeky crab gets up to a little harmless trouble now and then, Desktop Goose style. He never does it while you're clicking, on a call, guarding your focus, gaming or presenting, or while a task is running.

What he notices Off until you turn them on

Listening along

Shellby notices what's playing on this PC — Spotify, a browser tab, anything that shows up in the volume flyout — and puts his headphones on. Read from Windows itself: no account, and nothing leaves the PC.

Typing along

While you type, in any app, he taps along on a little keyboard, bobs to keep up when you're quick, and watches a long fast burst with stars in his eyes. He hears that a key was pressed, never which one, and none of it leaves the PC.

The weather outside

Pick your town and he dresses for the real weather: a sou'wester and umbrella in the rain, a bobble hat in the snow, shades on a hot afternoon. Every 30 minutes Shellby asks Open-Meteo (free, no account) about your town's rough position, to within about 11 km. It also tells him which half of the world you're in, so spring comes when yours does.
