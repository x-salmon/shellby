Time

Shellby can keep track of how long you spend on each project, for timesheets and invoices. He works it out from what he already sees:

  • The window in front. An editor or terminal showing a project's folder, or the project's page on GitHub.
  • Claude at work. While Claude works in a project, your terminal and docs count for it.
  • Git. A commit, checkout or pull says which project you're in.

The clock stops when you're away from the keyboard. Window titles are read to find the project and then forgotten: only the project, the day, the minutes and which quarter hours they fell in are kept, on this PC. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you send it to your time tracker, or switch on syncing your hours between your PCs.

Darker days had more hours. A dashed ring is a day with commits but no time kept.
