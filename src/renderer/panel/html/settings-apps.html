Each of these is off until you turn it on, and says what it reads and where anything goes.

Desk lighting

Put his mood on your keyboard and fans through OpenRGB: coral while he works, amber when he needs you, red when a build goes red, and orange running to red-hot when something overheats. Shellby can install it for you and starts it in the tray whenever the lighting is on.

    On your Discord profile

    Show his level and what he's up to under your name on Discord, the way a game does: Lv 12 Abyssal Admin · working with Claude Code. Shellby talks to the Discord app on this PC, so there's nothing to sign in to, and your friends list sees it while Shellby and Discord are both open.

    Never in Work mode. With visiting crabs on, there's a button to his calling card too.

    On a stream

    Serve Shellby as a browser source, on a transparent background, so he can sit in the corner of a stream and react while you work. It's the same crab, in the same outfit, on 127.0.0.1 only.

    —

    In OBS: + → Browser, paste that URL, and tick Shutdown source when not visible.

    Stream Deck

    Hardware keys for Allow, Deny, Stop, Bring it home and Ready to review, each showing what's waiting. Like answering from your phone, a question, a plan or anything the card would warn about says Look instead, and opens here. Needs Stream Deck 7.1 or newer.

    Stream Deck asks before it installs them. Then drag the keys you want from Shellby in its list of actions.

    Claude Code everywhere

    Shellby can react to Claude Code in your terminal or editor too: he works while Claude works, raises a claw when it needs permission, celebrates finished turns, and sends out helper crabs for subagents. That needs the Shellby plugin in Claude Code on this PC.
