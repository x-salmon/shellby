In-app notification: five directions

Today's toast is a centred pill under the header with a line draining along its bottom. Each direction below keeps what it already does (tones, buttons, pause while you're on it, a job that turns into its result) and changes how it looks and where it sits. The real messages come from the app.

1. Shellby says it

The notification comes from the crab, as a speech bubble above the box you type in. The title is in his pixel voice. It suits a crab who already talks; it's the most characterful of the five.

2. The tide line

A full-width band docked under the header instead of a floating pill. The time left is the water: the tint ebbs out of the band itself, not a thin line. Quiet, never covers the chat, and reads well on a wide panel.

4. The buoy

A compact chip in the top-right corner, out of the reading line. The time left runs around a ring instead of a bar, and the ring becomes the spinner for a job. One you haven't answered folds to a dot rather than vanishing.

Built for jobs: stepped pixel corners match the crab's world, and slow work shows its steps instead of one spinner. When it ends, a stamp says how. Plain messages use the same ticket without the steps.
