On your other PCs

Pages travel through the same private gist as the rest of Sync between PCs, each on its own, and arrive switched off.

Overview 1 The shelf 2 Running a page 3 Building with Claude 4 Agents and pages 5 Access and safety 6 On your phone 7 Other PCs

A page that arrives is code from a file on the internet, even when you wrote it yourself yesterday on your laptop. So it comes switched off, with its access not agreed, and runs only once you've looked at it here and said so. Everything else about sync is designed so that one page can never hold the rest up: each page goes on its own, a page too big stays home and says so, and a clash keeps both versions.
