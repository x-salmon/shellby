On your phone

A Pages tab in the phone app: the same page, in the same sandbox, with every call checked on your PC.

Overview 1 The shelf 2 Running a page 3 Building with Claude 4 Agents and pages 5 Access and safety 6 On your phone 7 Other PCs

The phone runs the page, your PC decides. The page's files come from the PC over your own network, and every bridge call goes back there to the same checks the desk uses, so a page can't do more from the sofa than it can at the desk. Anything the phone can't do honestly (run a desk-sized page, change a page while the PC sleeps) says so, rather than pretending.
