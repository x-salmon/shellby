Pages

Small apps you build with Claude, kept in Shellby, open on every screen you use, written to by your agents.

Overview 1 The shelf 2 Running a page 3 Building with Claude 4 Agents and pages 5 Access and safety 6 On your phone 7 Other PCs Full screen (4K)

Claude Code can make a small tool in a minute, and there's nowhere to keep one. A page is that tool with a home: you describe it, Claude builds it, and it stays. It sits on a shelf beside your conversations and opens beside a chat, on your phone and on your other PCs. Every conversation, routine and helper crab can read it and add to it. The plan is in shellby-pages.md; these are its screens.

Phase 3

Where the line is

A page is the first code a model wrote that Shellby runs. It gets no way out of its frame except one message channel, and every message is checked on the PC against what you agreed to, whether it came from the desk or the phone.
