On a wide panel

Full screen on 4K, the bubble stops at 640 px so a long message stays a readable paragraph instead of one line across the screen. Short ones stay short.

  • Words first. The message takes its natural width. If it fits beside the buttons they share a line; if not, the buttons wrap below, right-aligned, and wrap again if there are two long ones.
  • No length limit that hides words. Up to six lines show in full. Anything longer scrolls inside the bubble, and the time stops while you're reading it.
  • A title is optional. Most notifications are just a sentence, in body type. Only ones with a title get the pixel heading in the tone colour.
  • The crab carries the state. A count of what's waiting sits on his corner, a spinner rings him while a job runs, and he frowns when something failed. The colour of the bubble's edge says the tone (sea-glass, amber, red), so nothing depends on colour alone: the words say it too.
  • The ✕ never moves. It's pinned to the top-right corner, so it doesn't jump around as the bubble changes size.
