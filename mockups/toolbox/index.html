The toolbox, five ways

Today the toolbox is five sections, ten kinds and up to three rows of tabs before you see a list. Cost and removal live in both Tools and Lean, plugins are switched off in one place and installed in another, and the crew hides behind the Agents chip.

Each mockup below rebuilds all of it (Tools, Setup, Lean, Team, Get more and Crew) around one idea. They’re live: click, type, park things, play a turn. The size switch at the bottom right shows each one in the panel, a window, or full screen.
