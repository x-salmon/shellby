A rail down the side

All of today's settings, sorted into nine areas on a rail that never scrolls away. Each group's heading sticks while you're in it, and the chips at the top show only what you've changed, or the switches you haven't tried.

Things to try

  • Rest the cursor on a rail icon. A peek opens with that area's groups: click one to land right on it.
  • Change a few things in Code. Each gets a coral dot, the count lands on the rail, and the Changed chip fills up.
  • Pick Changed, then "Put these back". Change your mind with Undo.
  • Hover a changed row and press ↺ to put just that one back.
  • In Alerts, turn on "Send me these when I'm not at the desk" and watch its settings slide in.
  • Search "phone", "ssh" or "quieter". The rail dims the areas with nothing, and counts the rest. Enter opens the first.
  • Switch to Window or Full screen at the top right. The rail opens out with labels and lists the groups in the area you're in. Full screen adds a column of everything you've changed, in every area.
  • Keys: / or Ctrl+K to search, Alt+1–9 for an area, arrows along the rail and → into its peek. Back and Forward step through the areas you've visited.
