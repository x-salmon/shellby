Point at him

Settings is the beach. All of today's settings live on it: his shell, eyes, body, claw and legs each hold one area, and the five things on the sand hold the rest. Every change shows on him right away.

Things to try

  • Point at any part of him, then click. Arrow keys step through them too.
  • Or use the rail down the side. Hover an area there and that part of him lights up; point at him and the rail answers. Arrow keys work there too.
  • Change a setting and watch its count appear on the rail. Search, and the rail glows wherever the results are.
  • Switch to Window or Full screen at the top right. The beach widens to show more of the shore, the rail opens out with labels, and the areas sit two to a row.
  • Rub the cursor back and forth over his shell.
  • His shell: change his size and skin, then move his helpers.
  • His eyes: turn on music and the weather, then pick Rain or Sun.
  • His eyes: turn on typing along, then type fast in the search box.
  • His body: set mischief to Cheeky and bring the cursor near his claw.
  • His body: pick Rolling surf. Sound only starts once you click.
  • His claw: pick Autonomous.
  • Search "quieter", "phone", "model" or "ssh" and watch what lights up.
