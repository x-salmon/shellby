Tools › All, three ways

All is where most of the Toolbox lives: every skill, agent and command, 171 of them here. That’s 132 skills, 26 agents and 13 commands, and most of them came in plugins. These mockups use those real proportions.

Each one keeps everything All does today: search, where from, order, the source groups, the health line, picking many (Pin, Park, Export, Remove), Import, Use, the star, Chat as, row details and Parked. The notes beside each one say where every piece went. Once All is settled, Skills, Agents, Commands, MCP, Mods and Snippets follow. The size switch is at the bottom right.
